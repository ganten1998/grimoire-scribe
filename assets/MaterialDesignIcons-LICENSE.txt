Material Design Icons in Grimoire's Terminess

The 84 icons in assets/TerminessNerdFontMono-Regular.ttf and -Bold.ttf, the code points from
U+F0001 to U+F1AF0 that assets/terminess-keep.txt lists (the icons the app draws, on its tools,
buttons and sheets and as the clefs of music paper, and those its next version plans), are Material
Design Icons by Pictogrammers (https://pictogrammers.com/library/mdi/); 52 of them were drawn by
Google for Material Design. They come from the Material Design Icons font
(MaterialDesignIconsDesktop.ttf, https://github.com/Templarian/MaterialDesign-Font) as Nerd Fonts
3.4.0 carries it in src/glyphs/materialdesign/. That font is distributed under the
Apache License, Version 2.0, whose text is MaterialDesignIcons-Apache-2.0.txt, beside this file.

Changes: Nerd Fonts' font-patcher (https://github.com/ryanoasis/nerd-fonts) scaled and placed each
icon in the Terminess character cell when it made Terminess Nerd Font Mono 3.4.0. Grimoire then cut
that font down to Terminus's text glyphs and these icons (tools/fonts_subset.py in Grimoire's
source). The text glyphs are Terminus, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (Terminess-OFL-1.1.txt).
