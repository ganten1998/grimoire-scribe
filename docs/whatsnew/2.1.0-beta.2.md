# What's new (since 2.1.0-beta.1)

- PDFs become notebooks: drop one in grimoire/import, or pick it anywhere on the Kindle with Library > New > From a file.... A planner's tabs and page names come with it.
- Planner tabs work from KOReader too: Open this book in Grimoire brings the PDF's links, so a tap on "March" goes to March.
- Your own fonts: copy .ttf or .otf files into grimoire/fonts and choose one in Settings > Font (Classic).
- Closing a big notebook is much quicker: its PDF redraws only the pages you changed, and colour pages stay in colour.
- Lasso: drag a corner to make a selection bigger or smaller.
- Keyboard: ◀ and ▶ move the cursor, and the edit key selects, copies, cuts and pastes text.
- Settings > Wi-Fi switches between the Kindle's saved networks, and the clock can show 12-hour time.
- Holding the pen button no longer clears the page, and any gesture can now be set to do nothing.
- PDFs, pictures and fonts from outside Grimoire are now read in a separate, locked-down process.
- Names keep every character; one no font here can draw shows as a box.
- Thanks to nebuff for the lasso resize, the 12-hour clock, a faster Graph and the Japanese translation, now nearly complete.
