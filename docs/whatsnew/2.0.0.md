# What's new (since 2.0.0-beta.1)

- Pages: drag a page to reorder it, copy pages into another notebook, and hold + to add many at once.
- Lasso: select, copy, cut, paste and move ink, with an Exact area mode that cuts strokes along the lasso. Erasing and links travel with the ink. Hold a finger still on the page to lasso without the menu.
- Japanese, on every screen, with a flick kana keyboard.
- Keyboard: hold delete to delete quickly, and tap shift twice for caps.
- Settings > Shortcuts, with shortcuts of its own for each keyboard or controller, and Settings > Pair for Bluetooth controllers. One keyboard scheme everywhere: Esc goes back one step at a time.
- Live view: toasts show in the browser too, and picture in picture keeps up in large windows and in Firefox.
- Seyes paper is true to size (2 mm lines, 8 mm squares) and darker on the Scribe's screen.
- Quieter toasts, and fewer faint leftovers on the screen after moving the toolbar or erasing (issue #11, thanks nebuff).
- A smaller download: 2.9 MB, down from 7.1 MB.

Huge thanks to nebuff, who contributed the Japanese translation herself. She's a very talented contributor who has been onboarded to this endeavor, and will likely be contributing to the future of the project, as well as keeping a watchful eye on the backend where needed.

If you want to support the future of this project, please consider donating to the Ko-fi link in the GitHub.
