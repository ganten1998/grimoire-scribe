# What's new (since 2.0.0)

- PDFs become notebooks: drop one in grimoire/import, or pick it anywhere on the Kindle with Library > New > From a file.... A planner's tabs and page names come with it, from KOReader too.
- Fonts: Classic can use Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, Noto Sans, Literata or your own .ttf and .otf files from grimoire/fonts (Settings > Font).
- Quick sizes: hold a pen or the eraser on the toolbar to pick its size, or switch the eraser between Whole strokes and Precise.
- Faster: page turns no longer flash, turning back a page or two is instant, and closing a big notebook redraws only the pages you changed.
- Lasso: drag a corner to make a selection bigger or smaller, and the bar's buttons answer every tap.
- Keyboard: ◀ and ▶ move the cursor, and the edit key selects, copies, cuts and pastes text.
- Settings > Wi-Fi switches between the Kindle's saved networks, and the clock can show 12-hour time.
- Folders: moving or renaming a folder onto one with the same name offers Merge into it or Keep both.
- Holding the pen button no longer clears the page, and any gesture can be set to do nothing.
- PDFs, pictures and fonts from outside Grimoire are read in a separate, locked-down process.
- Thanks to nebuff for faster page turns, the lasso resize and fixes, the 12-hour clock and the Japanese translation.

If you want to support the future of this project, please consider donating to the Ko-fi link in the GitHub.
