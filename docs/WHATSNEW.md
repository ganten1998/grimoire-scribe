Grimoire was made with a lot of love and attention to detail. What I've aimed for is enabling
note-takers and artists to engage in a variety of workflows, while not overwhelming them with the
things that get in the way. It's tried and stress-tested design (I've been using it in my own life)
that is intuitive without sacrificing tweakability and personalization (hence two skins in the
settings menu, controller support, low latency screen monitoring, optional KOReader implementation,
etc.).

Learn the basic layout, then YOU choose when and how far to take your expression.

Open source and free, but I still need to eat. If you find value in projects like these, please
consider heading to the Ko-fi page linked on the GitHub. Taking constructive feedback and feature
requests, and there's already a roadmap ahead with no plans of stopping (accelerated by you, of
course), including:

- Sync and saveable profiles
- A fully fleshed out custom layout builder
- Big picture: Something I won't spoil, but it involves community. :)

Thanks to anyone who tried and gave feedback. I wasn't owed it, but it is a lot of the drive behind
what I hope will become a cozy home for anyone who decides to give it a shot.
